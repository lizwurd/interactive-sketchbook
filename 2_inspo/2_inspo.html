My name is Liz Contreras

These are the artists I am inspired by:


Luca Guadagnino
Photo: Yannis Drakoulidis / Metro Goldwyn Mayer Pictures

He is an Italian director, screenwriter, and producer. He is best known for his work on the films "Call Me
by Your Name" and "Challengers" Guadagnino’s passion and dedication to storytelling is told in these details.
It inspires me to think beyond basic creativity, it teaches me to include design in every layer.. Even in layers
I wouldn’t think to.

Saul Bass
Tony Barnard - Getty Images

He is an American graphic designer and film editor. He is best known for his work on the logos and opening
sequences of films such as "Vertigo" and "North by Northwest". His work changed graphic design forever, and his
timeless designs continue to inspire me to this day.

Tyler Childers
Scott Legato - Getty Images

He is an American country singer and songwriter. He is best known for his personal and vulnerable songwriting
style in the country music genre. His music inspires me to always feel deeply not only in my personal life but also
in whatever I create because it brings out something raw, real, and vulnerable.


Reflection: HTML is less scary now! I was like panicking because I was afraid I wasn't going to understand, but the moments where I got stuck were most rewarding because I figured out how to solve the issue. I feel like the three inspirations I chose may be from different creative outlets, but they all have being original and unique in common. They set the tone for their work and don't worry about meeting their audience's or peers' expectations. Alt text was bit challening because I want to be as inclusive as possible, but I also want to keep in mind that I should limit the details. I was having a hard time with the images at first because they weren't popping up! I realized it was because VSC was suggesting the code and would make up the name of the folder and image with what it assumed it was and not what it actually was. It was an easy fix, but kind of took time to realize what was happening. If I had more time, I would expirement with the CSS.
