about me

this is me


Hello, my name is Liz Contreras. I am from Southern California, but have been in San Francisco for about 10 years now. I am a visual communication design major at SFSU. I’ve loved design for a very long time but am just scratching the surface of the design world. I would say I need to do a lot more creating, but I would categorize myself as a creative appreciator or consumer right now.


Mainly, I love looking at design concepts, especially in typography and film. I would love to learn more before I start truly designing. The idea of creating a decent typeface or even a whole design campaign is a dream career for me. As most students, I am taking this class as a requirement. However, there’s been an interest in HTML since I was kid messing around on MySpace. Luckily, it’s related to graphic design, so I look forward to being able to create a web space for content I’ve created.
